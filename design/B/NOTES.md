# Direction B: paper-cut garden

1. Everything is a flat paper layer with a hard, unblurred offset shadow (static drop-shadow, never animated), like a picture-book diorama.
2. Silhouettes first: ink-green outlines on leaves, flowers and tiles so shapes read on a cheap phone in sunlight.
3. Palette is the passed one only. Sky, sun and leaf are saturated outside lessons. Inside, cream ground, three hues max (sky strip, one state colour, leaf underline). No red anywhere.
4. Right is teal border plus check badge; wrong is brown-orange border plus a wobble mark and "Not that one. Listen." Sprig tilts and points at one small example card.
5. Sprig: brown seed body, belly patch, one green leaf, dot eyes. Animated nodes: body breathe, leaf sway, eye blink (3). Lesson poses hold still.
6. Real keyframes: Sprig idle, watering drop, sprout pop (overshoot curve), slow sun rays (90 s). Transform and opacity only. prefers-reduced-motion stops all of them.
7. Heading font: Grandstander 700 (Google Fonts, one link, marked lazy-after-first-sprout). Chunky and rounded, single-storey a and g, and its a/s/t/m stay open at 76 px. Rejected Sniglet and Grandstander 800: counters close, the a turns into a blob.
8. Body text is the system stack. The same face sets the lesson letters, so one font file covers headings and graphemes (swap in Andika if the owner wants the literacy face).
9. Spec conflict found: DESIGN S4d caps the example card at 120 px and 30% of the feedback area. At a 232 px area that is a 69 px card, which I used (letter 50 px, under the 56 px rule). Suggest 40% / 96 px.
10. 12-node cap, what I cut first: the cloud drift (1), then the second pulsing ring on S1, then the glow on the second ready plant. Keep Sprig (3), watering drop, sun rays, one ready glow.
