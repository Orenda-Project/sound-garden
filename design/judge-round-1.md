## Overall

**Winner: A**, 3 of 5 screens, and the stronger reward and home loop. B wins the teaching moments.

**Biggest gap vs Duolingo:** Duolingo ties every answer to a picture, so the answer is self-evident. Sound Garden's letter-only questions make a non-reader memorise glyph shapes; the sound chip helps only after the tap.

**Three ideas to fold in from B:**
- Mascot points at the right answer and replays its sound on a wrong tap.
- The instruction sits on the target object ("Tap the seed").
- The home speech bubble is anchored to the plant that needs attention.

**Five fixes for A, by impact:**
1. Add a picture cue to every letter tile (sun for s) so non-readers can answer without reading.
2. Rework the wrong state: mascot points at the correct tile and plays its sound, instead of only "That was m."
3. Seed tap: enlarge the letter tiles and put the instruction on a sign at the seed; the tiles are tiny and the seed reads as a dirt pile.
4. Home: enlarge the letter labels on the stakes; they are the main learning cue and currently too small to read at phone width.
5. Sprout: replace the stock radial sunburst with a background drawn in the garden's illustration style.
